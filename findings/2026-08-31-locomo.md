# 2026-08-31 — LoCoMo, and what it says about the memory layer

## What was measured

LoCoMo (snap-research/locomo, CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial only) loaded into a
store of its own at `eval/locomo.db`: 10 conversations, 5,882 turns, 1,986
questions. Queried through `gate.answer_or_search` with a registered `eval`
caller, so admission, the access log and `relevance.narrow` all ran as they do
for a spoken question. Stratified sample, 40 per category, 200 questions,
seed 20260831. Judge is `claude-haiku-4-5` — deliberately not the model that
answers, so the score is not partly a measure of a model agreeing with itself.

End to end, judged / F1 / exact match:

- multi-hop 0.0% / 1.5% / 0.0%
- temporal 0.0% / 0.0% / 0.0%, and only 12.5% produced any answer
- open-domain 5.0% / 5.1% / 0.0%
- single-hop 10.0% / 13.0% / 2.5%
- four-category total 3.8% / 4.9% / 0.6%
- adversarial 97.5% correct — correctly declining a question the record does
  not answer. This is the category published comparisons drop, and it is the
  one this system wins.

Published systems sit around 60-70% on the four-category subset. 3.8% is not a
tuning gap.

## The diagnosis, which took three passes to get right

**First reading, wrong.** Retrieval recall of the gold evidence turn was 32.3%
at k=10 (`eval/locomo_recall.py`), so the index looked like the problem.

**Second reading, also wrong.** Of the 51 questions where the evidence *was*
retrieved, 3 produced a correct answer — 5.9% conditional accuracy, against
2.8% where it was not. Retrieving the right material barely helped, so the
composer looked like the problem, and the guards looked like the reason.

**What it actually was.** The composer was handed bare text. `gate.search_memory`
returns rows carrying `at` and `person`; every caller passed
`[h['text'] for h in hits]` and dropped both. So "when did Jon start reading
The Lean Startup" was answered from the line "I'm currently reading The Lean
Startup" with no date attached — and the gold answer, May 2023, *is* the
timestamp. Every temporal question was unanswerable by construction.

Measured directly: same 25 questions, same retrieved observations, lines
prefixed with date and speaker — **12% to 40%**. See
`2026-08-31-line-metadata.md`.

## What was rejected, and the number that rejected it

- **Loosening the grounding thresholds.** Swept `ANSWER_MIN_OVERLAP` 0.60 to
  0.20 against stem lengths 5, 4, 3. Conditional accuracy stayed at 2.0% at
  every one of the fifteen settings; emitted answers went from 17 to 80 and
  correct ones from 2 to 4. Rejected: 63 more refusals converted, 2 more
  correct answers, and a second adversarial case let through.
- **A larger composing model.** Routed `compose_answer` to `claude-opus-5` on
  the same 51 questions with the same lines: 2 correct against the on-device
  model's 1. Rejected as the explanation — the model is not the limit.
- **Running all 1,986 questions.** Sampled 200 on time, not cost: the full run
  is about four hours at 7.4s per question and under a dollar either way.

## Constants and where they came from

- `eval/locomo_run.py` `SEARCH_LIMIT = 10`, up from `gate.DEFAULT_SEARCH_LIMIT`
  of 5. Five is tuned for speech where an observation is four words; a LoCoMo
  turn is a paragraph, and multi-hop needs two pieces of evidence which five
  hits cannot hold alongside distractors.
- `eval/locomo_run.py` `RECENT_MINUTES = 0`, disabling the recent-speech rung.
  LoCoMo sessions are months apart and loaded in bulk, so "recent" would mean
  load order.
- `consolidate.EPISODE_GAP_S` unused, not set. It segments by silence and there
  is none in written dialogue. If consolidation is ever run over LoCoMo the
  boundaries should come from the session keys the data already carries.

## Cost

$0.366 for the 200-question run, 120 Claude calls plus 200 Haiku judge calls,
25 minutes, 7.4s per question. Ceilings in `capability.py` were raised at
runtime for this and have been made opt-in behind `--raise-limits` since.

## Assumed

- Category numbering (1 multi-hop, 2 temporal, 3 open-domain, 4 single-hop,
  5 adversarial) is from secondary sources; the repository ships no legend. Two
  examples were spot-checked.
- "Evidence retrieved" is a 60-character substring match either way, so recall
  figures are a lower bound.
- F1 normalisation is reimplemented SQuAD-style, not taken from
  `task_eval/evaluation.py`, which was never read.
- Conditional accuracy rests on 51 questions, roughly ±6 points.
